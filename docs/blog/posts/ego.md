# Ego

What makes yourself feel like yourself.
Can you observe that, and your reactions in an unbiased way?
Do you find it useful to question your ego?
Are there answers to believes which do not derive from ego, universal beliefs?

<!-- more -->

The idea for this series of thoughts came by while I was talking with a good friend Maxi, when asked me to explain what my meaning of life was and I tried to answer with [contentedness](posts/contentedness.md). It is a completely personal question and I think it can only be answered by observing your own ego.
